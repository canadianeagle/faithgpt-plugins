# Code of conduct

Be decent to each other.

This project touches Scripture and Christian teaching, and people who read the same Bible carefully disagree about baptism, church government, the gifts, and eschatology. Disagreement about those is expected here and is not a conduct problem. Contempt for the person on the other side of it is.

Not welcome: personal attacks, harassment, mocking someone's tradition or their lack of one, and dragging unrelated political fights into issues.

Report anything that needs attention to hello@faithgpt.io. Maintainers may edit, lock or remove contributions, and may block accounts.
